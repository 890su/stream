# Stream

Read first:

1. `CONTEXT.md` — canonical AI entrypoint and navigation.
2. `PLAN.md` — active tasks and blockers.
3. `CONCEPT.md` — concept, model, contracts and steps (source of truth).

## Gemini Rules

- Keep work scoped to the files referenced by `CONTEXT.md` unless the task
  requires more.
- Update `PLAN.md` after completed work.
- New durable decisions go to `decisions.md`.
- Never edit `ROADMAP.md` unless explicitly asked.
- Respect `.aiignore` and `.rai.yaml`.
- Never read `.library/`, `data/` or secret files unless explicitly instructed and safe.
